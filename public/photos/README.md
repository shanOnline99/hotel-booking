# Photos Directory Structure

Drop your resort image files in the subfolders below:

- `public/photos/hero/` — Hero banner & background images (e.g. `/photos/hero/hero-bg.jpg`)
- `public/photos/rooms/` — Room & villa interior/exterior photos (e.g. `/photos/rooms/ocean-villa.jpg`)
- `public/photos/gallery/` — Resort photo gallery images (e.g. `/photos/gallery/safari.jpg`)
- `public/photos/amenities/` — Spa, pool, dining, and experience photos (e.g. `/photos/amenities/pool.jpg`)
- `public/photos/location/` — Map & location arrival photos (e.g. `/photos/location/map.jpg`)

Supported image formats: `.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.png`, `.webp`, `.svg`
